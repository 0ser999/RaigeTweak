Refund Policy

Information about refunds and returns for Raige Tweak

Last Updated:

Important: All sales of Raige Tweak software are final. We do not offer refunds for purchased licenses unless required by law or in exceptional circumstances as outlined below.

1. No Refund Policy

Due to the digital nature of our software and the immediate delivery of license keys upon purchase, we maintain a strict no-refund policy. Once a license key has been generated and delivered, the sale is considered final.

2. Exceptions

Refunds may be considered in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Duplicate Purchase: If you accidentally purchase the same product twice, we will refund one of the purchases.
  • Technical Issues: If the software is completely non-functional due to a technical issue on our end that cannot be resolved.
  • Unauthorized Purchase: If your payment method was used without your authorization, contact us immediately.

3. Refund Request Process

If you believe you qualify for a refund under the exceptions listed above, please:

  1. Contact us at support@raigetweak.com within 7 days of purchase
  2. Provide your order number or license key
  3. Explain the reason for your refund request
  4. Include any relevant documentation or screenshots

We will review your request and respond within 5-7 business days.

4. Refund Processing

If your refund is approved:

  • Refunds will be processed to the original payment method
  • Processing may take 5-10 business days
  • Your license key will be deactivated
  • You must stop using the software immediately

5. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback through your payment provider without first contacting us, we reserve the right to:

  • Permanently ban your account
  • Deactivate all associated license keys
  • Dispute the chargeback with supporting documentation

6. Subscription Cancellations

If you have a subscription (if applicable in the future), you may cancel at any time. Cancellation will take effect at the end of your current billing period. No refunds will be provided for the current billing period.

7. Free Trial Period

If we offer a free trial period in the future, you may cancel before the trial ends to avoid charges. No refunds are provided for free trial periods.

8. Contact Us

For refund inquiries, please contact us at:

Email: support@raigetweak.com

Subject: Refund Request - [Your Order Number]

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.
