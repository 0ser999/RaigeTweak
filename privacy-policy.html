Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

Last Updated:

1. Introduction

Raige Tweak ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our software and services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Email address and account credentials
  • Payment information (processed securely through Stripe)
  • License key information
  • Support communications

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • System information (OS version, hardware specifications)
  • Usage data and performance metrics
  • IP address and device identifiers
  • Application logs and error reports

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Process payments and manage licenses
  • Send important notifications and updates
  • Improve our software and user experience
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as:

  • Stripe: Payment processing (see Stripe's privacy policy)
  • Google: Authentication services (see Google's privacy policy)
  • Email Services: For sending verification codes and receipts

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@raigetweak.com
